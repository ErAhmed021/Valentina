About us

About Valentina Precious

Valentina Precious is an activewear and lifestyle brand built around one idea: clothing that supports you from your workout to the rest of your day. Its own words are “comfort, confidence, and style”.

The range is deliberately focused: sports bras, tops, jackets and bottoms, grouped into sets so pieces can be mixed and matched. Signature collections include the Contour and Drift sets.

The brand is led by founder Amol Naresh. [Further purpose and positioning copy pending verified material from the owner.]
